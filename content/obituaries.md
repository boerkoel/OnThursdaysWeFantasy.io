# Obituaries

Handwritten obituaries for teams chopped from the guillotine league. They
appear in the Rest in Peace section of the site after you commit this file
(editing it on github.com works too).

How to add one:

- Add a section whose `##` heading is the team's name exactly as it appears
  on ESPN (capitalization doesn't matter).
- Write the obituary under the heading. Leave a blank line between
  paragraphs.
- Anything inside `<!-- -->` comments is ignored, so notes and the facts
  below won't show up on the site.
- A team with no section, or an empty one, shows "Obituary pending."

His team managed just 77.55 points, finishing 12.17 behind the next-lowest scorer. A 1.8-point Monday night performance ensured Jim was Jaxson Darted out of the league before the season even got going. Meanwhile, 31.4 points sat on the bench watching the carnage unfold. ## Plan B

Here lies Plan B, the fantasy football experiment that proved Kevin Simpson should have stuck with Plan A: literally anything else.

Kevin’s squad finally met its maker, falling to a pedestrian 119.7 points after Colston Loveland apparently decided that scoring more than a single point per game was a reasonable career goal. One touchdown, one funeral. Somewhere, Kevin is screaming into the void, wondering if he should’ve gone with Plan Casket.

Joe Burrowed himself six feet under, Bucky Irving ran out of luck, and De’Von Achane couldn’t outrun the Grim Reaper. Chris Olave’d his last goodbye, while Ladd McConkey couldn’t connect to a lifeline. Breece Hall stumbled into the afterlife, and poor Tee Higgins couldn’t catch a break. Meanwhile, Malik Nabers discovered that nobody visits you in the fantasy graveyard.

Kevin valued depth at draft time and his bench obliged--outscoring his starters just to dig him a deeper grave. Kevin’s bench offered no Warren of hope, and Dallas Goedert had nothing good to report.

Plan B now rests in pieces, its FAAB dreams buried alongside its dignity.

Rest in pieces, Kevin. There is no Plan C.

## Stacey's Smart Team

“It Wasn’t Very Smart”

We gather today to mourn Stacey Mette and her tragically poorly named fantasy football team, whose life was cut short by the cruelest weapon in the league: a razor-thin margin of defeat.

There once was a manager, Mette,   
Whose roster was limp as spaghetti.   
With Diggs looking ill   
And Kincaid standing still,   
She got chopped, and the league threw confetti.

Josh Allen did his part, but Cook couldn’t cook. Skattebo took the wrong road, McBride never found his groove, and even Golden couldn’t strike gold.

Stacey didn’t even have the decency to wait for Monday Night Football. No, she sealed her own fate during the afternoon slate—an early death wish in every sense of the word.

Meanwhile, the executioner fielded Terry McLaurin—a literal zombie starter—and STILL managed to survive.

That’s right: Stacey’s season was ended by a team so devoid of identity it doesn’t even have a name.

Let that sink in.

A nameless team.
A zombie starter.
And somehow… Stacey is the one being buried.

May she rest in peace—and may her roster rest in waivers.

## Moose Knucklers
Billy Got Kittled.

Billy Halverson saw it coming. “My team’s been circling the drain for weeks now,” he admitted last night.

But nobody expected the end to come this way.

While attempting to wheel the guillotine across the street, Billy tragically failed to look both ways. One freak accident later, his fantasy life was over.

The irony? He survived weeks of fantasy football torture, only to get decapitated by a Purdy boy making a pass.

Love abandoned him. Henry couldn't carry him. Hurts was hurt. Saquon was gone. And when the chips were down, Brock Bowers couldn't build a bridge back to the living.

As for the cause of death? A tight end.

Billy may have worn tight shorts that left his Moose Knuckle impossible to ignore, but let's face it: Joe's tight end was what finally Kittled him. George Kittle didn't just catch a pass. He delivered the Kittle coup de grâce.

Billy's teaching career may continue, but his fantasy career has been officially dismissed.

Rest in pieces, Moose Knucklers.

The team is dead. The guillotine is in the shop. And Joe's tight end is still getting talked about in the teachers' lounge.

Class dismissed. ☠️
